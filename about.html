Our Story

Beauty That Gives Back

Founded in 2014, GlowAura began with a simple belief: skincare should be gentle, effective, and kind to the planet.

10+
Years of
Clean Beauty

From a kitchen to a global glow

We started in a small kitchen with hand-poured formulas, driven by a passion for clean ingredients and transparent beauty. Every batch was tested on ourselves first — never on animals, never with harsh synthetics.

Today, every GlowAura product is still crafted with the same care — free from parabens, sulfates, and synthetic fragrances. We source sustainably, test ethically, and formulate scientifically.
